Visorec

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Visorec is an app for recording your phone's screen. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

Controller: arnMind, developer of Visorec. Contact: forja.arnmind@gmail.com. The owner's legal details are listed on Visorec's Google Play page.

What you record

  • The screen is recorded only when you ask, and Android confirms it with its own notice. While you record, a visible notification says so.
  • Videos are saved on your phone, in the Movies/Visorec folder, like any other video: you see them in your gallery and in your files. While recording, the app keeps a working copy in its private space on the phone and deletes it when it finishes.
  • Videos are never sent to the developer or to third parties. They only leave the phone if you share them.
  • Everything on the screen while you record (messages, notifications, passwords, other people's details) ends up in the video. Check it before sharing it. Some apps, such as banking or video streaming apps, don't allow their screen to be recorded and show up black.

Sound and camera

  • The microphone and the phone's sound are recorded only if you choose so. The sound goes inside the video and is not stored anywhere else. Calls can't be recorded.
  • The front camera (Visorec Pro) is used only if you turn on the bubble, and only while you record. Its picture goes inside the video.

What Visorec doesn't do

  • It has no user accounts and no servers of its own.
  • It has no ads, analytics or trackers, and it doesn't sell or share data.
  • It doesn't access your location, your contacts or your photos.

Purchases

The Visorec Pro subscription is bought and managed on Google Play. Visorec doesn't receive or store your card details; it only asks Google Play whether the subscription is active. Google handles the purchase data under its own privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Record the screen: Android asks every time you start a recording.
  • Microphone: to record your voice or the phone's sound (Android requires this permission for both).
  • Camera (Pro): for the bubble with your face.
  • Notifications: to show the time, pause and stop while you record, and the video saved notice.
  • Display over other apps (optional): for the floating bar, the countdown and the camera bubble.
  • Internet: only to check your subscription with Google Play.
  • Storage (Android 8 and 9 only): to save videos in the Movies folder.

How long and how to delete it

Your videos stay on your phone until you delete them from the app, the gallery or the file manager. The app's settings are deleted when you uninstall it. Visorec is not included in Android's backup and makes no cloud copies.

Since Visorec collects no data about you, the developer has no data to give you, correct or delete. Even so, for any question about your data protection rights, write to forja.arnmind@gmail.com. If you think we haven't handled your request well, you can complain to the data protection authority of your country; in Spain, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (aepd.es).

Children

Visorec is not intended for children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If the policy changes, the new version will appear here and on the Google Play page with its update date.
