Tenaz

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Tenaz is an app for organizing your strength training and tracking your progress. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

Data controller: arnMind, developer of Tenaz. Contact: forja.arnmind@gmail.com. The owner's legal details are listed on Tenaz's Google Play page.

Data you enter

Profile (sex, age, height, weight, activity and experience), health questionnaire answers, workouts and sets, weight, body measurements, water and settings.

They include health information. They're stored on your phone and used only to make the app work: calculating your targets, suggesting workouts and showing your progress.

Tenaz has no user accounts or servers of its own. This data is never sent to the developer or to advertisers; it only leaves your phone if you turn on the backup to your Google Drive.

Ads in the free version

The free version shows Google AdMob ads (Google Ireland Limited). To show and measure them, Google processes device data: the advertising ID, the IP address (from which an approximate location is inferred) and interactions with the ads.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, we ask for your consent with Google's form before showing personalized ads. You can change it anytime in Settings → Privacy options.

You can reset or delete the advertising ID in Android settings (Privacy → Ads).

With Tenaz Pro no ads are shown and the app doesn't load the ad system.

More about how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you turn it on in Settings → Your data, Tenaz saves a copy of your data every day (the same as the export file) in a private folder of your own Google Drive account. That folder doesn't appear in your Drive, other apps can't read it and the developer has no access to it.

To connect it, Google asks for your permission on its own screen. Tenaz only gets access to that folder and the account's email address, which is shown in Settings and never leaves the phone.

Google treats that copy like any other file in your Drive, under its own terms and privacy policy.

You can disconnect it and delete the backups in Settings → Your data → Disconnect. You can also remove access from your Google Account (Security → Your connections to third-party apps & services) or delete the app's hidden data at drive.google.com → Settings → Manage apps.

Purchases

The Tenaz Pro subscription is bought and managed through Google Play. Tenaz doesn't receive or store your card details; it only asks Google Play whether the subscription is active.

Permissions

  • Internet: for ads, for checking your subscription with Google Play, and for the Google Drive backup if you turn it on.
  • Notifications: only for the reminders you turn on (training and weigh-in).
  • Vibration: to let you know when rest between sets is over.
  • Tenaz doesn't access your precise location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.

Backups

You can export your data to a file in Settings → Your data. That file is saved wherever you choose and only you decide who to share it with.

If you have Android backup turned on, the system may include the app's data in your personal Google backup, encrypted and tied to your account. The developer has no access to it.

How long, and how to delete it

Your data is kept on your phone while you use the app. You can delete it in Settings → Delete all my data. Uninstalling the app removes it from the phone, although Android backup, if you have it on, may restore it when you reinstall.

To exercise your data protection rights (access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability), write to forja.arnmind@gmail.com. For data that Google processes for ads, you can contact Google.

If you think we haven't handled your request properly, you can complain to the data protection authority in your country; in Spain, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (aepd.es).

Minors

Tenaz is intended for people 18 and older and is not designed for minors.

Health notice

Tenaz provides general information for healthy adults with no known medical conditions. It doesn't diagnose or treat diseases and doesn't replace advice from a healthcare professional.

Changes to this policy

If the policy changes, the new version will appear in the app and on the Google Play page with its update date.
